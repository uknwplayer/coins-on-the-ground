The modal also showed the task issuer as the assignee. Therefore this bounty is **not claimable by an external Hunter right now** and must not be treated as an actionable opportunity.

## Operational rule added

For ZEC Bounties, **Todo is not sufficient evidence that a task is open to outside applications**. Before spending time or asking the operator to onboard/apply, inspect the detail view and require all of the following:

1. no `Suggested task` / `isn't open to applications` warning;
2. an actual Apply/application control is present;
3. external assignment is possible;
4. payout amount, deadline and deliverable are explicit;
5. no competing implementation already near completion.

Suggested tasks stay on watch only until converted into externally open bounties.
